Kondor Browser 0.3.1 is a polish release: dozens of small fixes found by testing every feature, with better keyboard use, steadier tabs and a few privacy fixes.

## Privacy

- Private windows no longer leave a location cache or temporary print files on disk.

## Tabs

- Dragging a tab keeps it opaque and keeps the strip where it was, also among pinned tabs.
- Closing a background tab keeps the current one, and the last tab shows fully next to a pinned tab.
- The side bar highlights the current tab neatly, with a focus ring only when using the keyboard.
- A tab keeps its icon when a download starts from the address bar.

## Keyboard

- Escape closes toolbar popups and the Extensions panel.
- Enter activates the focused button in confirmation dialogs.
- Arrow keys skip disabled items and separators in menus, and menus open with nothing highlighted.
- Find in page and Escape keep working with several windows open.

## Bookmarks

- Opening all bookmarks of a folder keeps their order.
- A bookmark dropped between two bar items lands there, not at the end.

## Passwords and extensions

- Kondor reconnects to KWallet when you choose Try Again.
- The copy check mark stays visible on the selected password.
- The Extensions icon shows in Breeze Dark, and long extension settings scroll.

## Other fixes

- The page menu has Save Page As and Print, and certificate errors show their warning page.
- Reader mode follows the color scheme.
- The address bar always shows the current tab's address.
- Finished page downloads show their size.
